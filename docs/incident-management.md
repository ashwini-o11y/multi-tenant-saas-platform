# Incident Management

This document establishes an intended incident-management direction for a future operational service. It does not establish on-call coverage or operational readiness today.

Future incidents should have a clear severity model, incident lead, communication path, and record of timeline, impact, decisions, and recovery actions. Response should prioritize user impact and safe restoration. Follow-up should identify contributing conditions, corrective actions, owners, and due dates without treating blame as root cause.

As telemetry and SLOs arrive, alerts should be actionable and linked to service ownership and runbooks. SARI may later help correlate signals and recommend a remediation, but human approval and verifiable outcomes remain explicit controls.
